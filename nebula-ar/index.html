clouds splits the gas into that many separate, smaller clouds; the second takes the complementary hue (+180°) and later ones fan out from there. arrangement reshuffles where they sit. clumping makes some regions of a cloud far denser than others (clump size sets how big those regions are), and colour spread widens the hue gradient and varies it across the cloud instead of only where it is dense. The nebula is placed 1.5 m ahead when tracking starts — walk around it. "Place here" drops it in front of you again. Benchmark measures 8s including the camera feed, which is the number that matters. 1% low is the worst frames.
