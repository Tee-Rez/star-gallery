In Volume + Particles the gas is a single cloud marched through procedural fBm, so it keeps fine detail at any distance, and clusters sets how many particle groups sit inside it — the second takes the complementary hue (+180°), later ones fan out. On the 3D texture tab, clouds splits the gas itself. clumping makes some regions far denser than others; colour spread varies hue across the cloud instead of only where it is dense. Benchmark measures 8s including the camera feed. Keep cover similar between runs or the numbers are not comparable.
