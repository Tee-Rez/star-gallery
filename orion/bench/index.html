Starsong bench

Auditions the real modules the app ships — star-tone.js, star-physics.js and star-voice.js — so what you hear here is what the AR build will play. Sorted low to high: the largest stars sound lowest.

Musical is what ships: pitches compressed across this constellation's own range and snapped to a pentatonic scale, so any combination stays consonant. True is Aerts's method — the real oscillation frequency multiplied by 10⁵ with no snapping. Physics here is estimated from each star's spectral class, so tones are characteristic of the star's type rather than measured from that individual star.
