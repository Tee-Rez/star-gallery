# Lore writing style

Rules for every journey stop and star description in this app. They exist because the
default register of generated prose is recognisable, and the lore is the one part of this
project that has to sound like a person wrote it.

Derived from published analyses of AI writing tells (see Sources below), then applied to
what this project actually has: a vault full of specific Arabic names, page citations,
cuneiform transliterations and dates. Specificity is the main defence, and we have plenty.

## Do not

**No negated contrast.** "It's not X, it's Y", "not merely X but Y". This is the single
most-cited tell. If a comparison matters, state both halves plainly.

**No tricolons.** Three parallel phrases of near-identical length, especially as a closer.
Two items or four are fine. Watch for accidental threes in lists of cultures.

**No metronome.** Generated prose sits at 14–22 words a sentence with little variance.
Break it on purpose: follow a thirty-word sentence with a five-word one. Some paragraphs
should open with a fragment.

**No additive em-dashes.** An em-dash is for interruption, not for bolting a qualifier onto
a finished sentence. At most one per stop, and only where a comma would genuinely misread.

**No inflated vocabulary.** Banned outright: delve, underscore, showcase, pivotal, intricate,
meticulous, tapestry, landscape (figurative), realm, mosaic, ecosystem, robust, seamless,
transformative, groundbreaking, profound, testament, "rich history", "stands as", "serves as
a reminder", "at its core", "weave"/"woven" (figurative).

**No scaffolding.** No opening that announces what the stop will cover, no closing sentence
that restates it. Start on the substance and stop when it runs out.

## Do

**Name things exactly.** *Al-Nitāq*, not "an Arabic word". *MUL.APIN*, c. 1000 BCE, not
"ancient Mesopotamian texts". Allen 1899 p. 383, not "historical sources". The vault holds
this detail already; use it.

**Let the cultures disagree.** Where two traditions read the same stars differently, give
both readings their own sentences and let the difference stand without a moral.

**Keep the seams visible.** Where a name is the result of a scribal error, a
misattribution, or an administrative decision, say so. Those are the best stories and they
are all documented.

**Attribute inference.** The vault marks its own inferences as Synthesis. Anything not in a
cited source is written as an observation, not as scholarship, or left out.
