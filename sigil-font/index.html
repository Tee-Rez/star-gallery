Sigil Inscriptional

A display face for the temple HUD. Every glyph is a few straight lines on a 10-unit grid, so the whole alphabet is about 2 KB of data - and the weight, the lozenge nodes and the terminal cuts are parameters rather than drawings.

Carved

The groove is shaded from the glyph's own distance field: depth falls off from the centre of the stroke, a normal is taken from that depth, and the light is a single directional source you can swing with Light. Nothing is baked - the same letters can be a shallow scratch or a deep channel with gold in the bottom of it.

The alphabet

At HUD sizes

The same string at 11, 14, 18, 26 and 40 px. The lozenges are the first thing to go at small sizes - below about 13 px set Node to 0 and let the terminals square off.

Readability check

The pairs that break a display face: O/Q/D/0, I/l/1, E/F, C/G, S/5, B/8, U/V, M/W, K/X. If any two are hard to tell apart here, the skeleton is wrong, not the styling.
