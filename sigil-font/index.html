Sigil Inscriptional

Angular capitals with one small alchemical mark each. Four shapes do all the work - a diamond for closed bowls, a triangle for open ones, a chevron for open letters, a bracket for flat feet - and the numerals are built from the same four, which is why they sit with the letters instead of beside them.

Carved

The groove is shaded from the glyph's own distance field: a V section whose depth falls from the centre of the stroke, an analytic normal taken from that depth, one directional light, and gold pooled only in the deepest part of the cut. Nothing is baked, so the same letters are a shallow scratch or a deep channel depending on where you put Depth.

The alphabet

At HUD sizes

11, 14, 18, 26 and 40 px - the sizes your DOM HUD actually uses. The marks are the first thing to go; below about 14 px the ramp drops them automatically, which is the text cut.

Readability check

The pairs that break a display face. O/0 is settled by the dot inside the diamond; B/3 and S/5 are the two to keep watching.

The compiled font

Real HTML text in the compiled SigilInscriptional-Regular.ttf. If this renders, the DOM HUD and Unity both have what they need.
